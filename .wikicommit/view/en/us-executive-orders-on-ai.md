United States presidents have used executive orders to set federal policy on artificial intelligence. This page puts the orders recorded in the wiki, and the dated events around them, into one order: from [[Legislation/executive-order-14110|Executive Order 14110]] in October 2023, through its rescission and replacement in January 2025, to the orders of December 2025 and June 2026. The detail of each order stays on its own page:

- [[Legislation/executive-order-14110|Executive Order 14110]], "Executive Order on Safe, Secure, and Trustworthy Development and Use of Artificial Intelligence" (Joe Biden, 30 October 2023)
- [[Legislation/executive-order-14179|Executive Order 14179]], "Removing Barriers to American Leadership in Artificial Intelligence" (Donald Trump, 23 January 2025)
- [[Legislation/executive-order-14365|Executive Order 14365]], "Ensuring a National Policy Framework for Artificial Intelligence" (Donald Trump, December 2025)
- [[Legislation/executive-order-14409|Executive Order 14409]], "Promoting Advanced Artificial Intelligence Innovation and Security" (Donald Trump, June 2026)

## Before October 2023

Executive Order 14110 was the third executive order dealing explicitly with AI; the two earlier ones were signed by Donald Trump during his first term. Earlier in 2023 the Biden administration had announced a Blueprint for an AI Bill of Rights and secured non-binding AI safety commitments from major technology companies, and in August 2023 the director of the White House Office of Science and Technology Policy indicated that the White House was expediting its work on executive action on AI.

## 30 October 2023: Executive Order 14110

Joe Biden signed [[Legislation/executive-order-14110|Executive Order 14110]], which defined the administration's policy goals for AI and ordered executive agencies to take actions toward them. It has been described as the most comprehensive piece of governance by the United States regarding AI.

## 20 January 2025: rescission of Executive Order 14110

Hours after his inauguration as the 47th president, Donald Trump rescinded Executive Order 14110 through the "Initial Rescissions of Harmful Executive Orders and Actions" executive order.

## 23 January 2025: Executive Order 14179

Trump signed [[Legislation/executive-order-14179|Executive Order 14179]] as the replacement order covering the development of AI technologies. Among its provisions was a mandated action plan to sustain U.S. AI leadership. On behalf of the Office of Science and Technology Policy, the Networking and Information Technology Research and Development program requested public input on the development of the AI Action Plan, and over 10,000 public comments were submitted.

## March 2025: OpenAI's preemption proposal

OpenAI proposed that the administration preempt pending state AI laws with federal law.

## May 2025: the proposed ban on state AI laws

House Republicans inserted a clause banning state AI laws for 10 years into a tax and spending bill. The Senate defeated the clause by a 99–1 vote, whose date the wiki's pages do not give.

## December 2025: Executive Order 14365

Trump signed [[Legislation/executive-order-14365|Executive Order 14365]], which seeks to preempt state regulation of AI.

## March 2026: legislative recommendations

The White House published "A National Legislative Policy Framework for Artificial Intelligence: Legislative Recommendations", asking Congress to implement the administration's AI policy strategy through federal legislation.

## June 2026: Executive Order 14409

Trump issued [[Legislation/executive-order-14409|Executive Order 14409]], which directs federal agencies on federal cybersecurity, AI-enabled defensive tools, a voluntary framework for the secure deployment of frontier AI models, and enforcement against AI-enabled cybercrime. In the same month the Department of Commerce extended export controls beyond AI hardware by requiring licenses for certain frontier AI models from Anthropic.
