```text
READER QUESTIONS (Legislation)
Readers: practitioners, compliance staff, and researchers who need to know whether a law
touches AI systems or security work they are responsible for, and what it asks of them.
1. What is this instrument — its kind (act, regulation, directive, order, bill), who issued
   it, for which jurisdiction, and whether it is proposed, adopted, in force, or repealed?
2. What does it cover — which systems, activities, or organisations fall within its scope,
   and which are explicitly excluded?
3. What does it require or prohibit, as the source states it (a table when there are several
   obligations that differ by role or risk level)?
4. When do its obligations apply — key dates, phase-in periods, and transitional provisions?
5. How is it enforced — the authorities responsible, the penalties, and any enforcement so far?
6. What does it mean specifically for the security of AI systems or for AI used in security
   work, as opposed to its general provisions?
7. How does it relate to other laws, and to the voluntary frameworks or standards it cites or
   relies on?
8. For a proposed or contested instrument only: where does it stand in the legislative
   process, and what are the main points of disagreement?
```
