```text
READER QUESTIONS (Project)
Readers: practitioners, policy staff and researchers who met the initiative's name in a
policy document or news report and want to know what it is and whether it matters to them.
1. What is this initiative — what kind of enterprise it is, who launched it, when, and
   whether it is still running?
2. What is it meant to achieve — its stated aims and the problem it responds to?
3. Who takes part in it, and in what roles?
4. What has it produced or agreed so far — documents, principles, commitments, tools — and
   when (a table when there are several)?
5. How does it work — its structure, the level at which it operates, and whether what it
   produces is binding or voluntary?
6. How does it stand among related initiatives, laws or frameworks the source mentions?
7. How has it been received or assessed, as the source reports it?
```
