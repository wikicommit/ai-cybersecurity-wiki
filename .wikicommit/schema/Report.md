READER QUESTIONS (Report)
Readers: practitioners, analysts and policy staff who met the report cited elsewhere, or who
want to know what an organisation has said on a subject and how much weight to give it.
1. What is this report — who issued it, when, in what form (assessment, advisory, guidelines),
   and for whom it is written?
2. What does it set out to do — the question it addresses or the problem it responds to?
3. What are its main findings, judgements or recommendations, as the report states them (a
   table when there are several along the same dimensions)?
4. How does it reach them — the evidence, method or confidence language it uses, if it says?
5. What does it advise readers or organisations to do?
6. Where does it stand among the issuer's other reports and the frameworks or laws it cites —
   what it builds on, replaces or is aligned with?
7. How has it been received, used or criticised, as the source reports it?
