## Summary

Careful adoption of agentic AI services is joint guidance co-authored by ASD's ACSC, CISA, the NSA, the Canadian Centre for Cyber Security, NCSC-NZ and NCSC-UK on the security of agentic AI systems, primarily those based on large language models. It explains how agentic AI inherits LLM risks such as prompt injection while widening the attack surface and adding complexity, groups its specific risks into privilege, design and configuration, behaviour, structural and accountability risks illustrated with scenarios, and sets out best practices for designing, developing, deploying and operating agents securely. It advises organisations to align agentic AI with their existing security model, never grant it broad access, use it only for low-risk and non-sensitive tasks, deploy it incrementally, and prioritise resilience, reversibility and risk containment until practices and standards mature.
