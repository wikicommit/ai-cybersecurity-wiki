## Summary

The March 2025 edition (NIST AI 100-2e2025) of NIST's taxonomy and terminology of adversarial machine learning, written with authors from NIST, the U.S. AI Safety Institute, the U.K. AI Security Institute, Northeastern University and Cisco. It classifies attacks on predictive AI systems (evasion, poisoning and privacy attacks) and on generative AI systems (supply chain, direct prompting and indirect prompt injection attacks) by learning stage and the attacker's goals, capabilities and knowledge, assigns each attack class an identifier in a taxonomy index, and describes mitigations and their limitations. It adds discussions of the security of AI agents and of benchmarks for AML vulnerabilities, and closes with open challenges and a glossary.
