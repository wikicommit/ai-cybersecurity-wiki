## Summary

NIST AI 600-1, published by the US National Institute of Standards and Technology in July 2024, is a cross-sectoral profile of and companion resource to the AI Risk Management Framework (AI RMF 1.0) for generative AI, developed under Executive Order 14110. It defines twelve risks that are novel to or exacerbated by generative AI — from CBRN information and confabulation to information security and value chain integration — and maps each to the AI RMF's trustworthiness characteristics. It then lists suggested actions to govern, map, measure and manage those risks, organised by AI RMF subcategory, and closes with four primary considerations from NIST's Generative AI Public Working Group: governance, pre-deployment testing, content provenance and incident disclosure.
