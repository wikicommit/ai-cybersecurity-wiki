## Summary

A NIST Trustworthy and Responsible AI report, dated January 2024, that develops a taxonomy and terminology of adversarial machine learning, built from a survey of the literature. It classifies attacks on predictive AI systems (evasion, poisoning and privacy attacks) and on generative AI systems (adding supply-chain, direct and indirect prompt injection and abuse attacks) by the learning stage they target, the attacker's goals, capabilities and knowledge, describes the corresponding mitigations and their limitations, discusses open challenges, and includes a glossary of key terms.
