## Summary

NIST SP 800-218A, published by the US National Institute of Standards and Technology in July 2024, is an SSDF Community Profile that augments the practices and tasks of the Secure Software Development Framework (SSDF) version 1.1 with recommendations, considerations, notes and informative references specific to developing generative AI and dual-use foundation models. Prepared as the companion resource that Executive Order 14110 asked NIST to develop, it is aimed at AI model producers, AI system producers and AI system acquirers, covers AI model development (data sourcing, design, training, fine-tuning, evaluation and integration) but not deployment and operation, and is meant to be used together with NIST SP 800-218 rather than on its own. Its main table lists the SSDF practices and tasks across four groups — Prepare the Organization, Protect Software, Produce Well-Secured Software and Respond to Vulnerabilities — with a priority for each task and mappings to the AI RMF, the OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications and NIST's adversarial machine learning taxonomy.
