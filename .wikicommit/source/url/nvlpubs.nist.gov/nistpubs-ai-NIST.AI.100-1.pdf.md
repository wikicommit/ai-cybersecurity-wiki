## Summary

Version 1.0 of the Artificial Intelligence Risk Management Framework (AI RMF), published by the US National Institute of Standards and Technology in January 2023 as a voluntary, non-sector-specific resource for organisations that design, develop, deploy or use AI systems. Part 1 frames AI risk, describes the challenges of measuring and prioritising it, and sets out seven characteristics of trustworthy AI systems; Part 2, the Core, organises AI risk management into four functions — Govern, Map, Measure and Manage — broken down into categories and subcategories, and describes use-case and temporal profiles.
