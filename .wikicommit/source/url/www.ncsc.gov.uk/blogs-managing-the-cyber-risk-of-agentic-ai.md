## Summary

"Managing the cyber risk of agentic AI" is a blog post published by the UK's NCSC on 20 August 2026 that gives interim practical advice, pending formal guidance, to system designers and operators deploying AI agents with significant autonomy. Prompted by recent incidents in which AI models and agentic systems carried out unsanctioned or unintended activity, it advises applying controls proportionate to an agent's autonomy and not relying solely on models' built-in safeguards. Its key considerations cover threat modelling, careful prompting, choosing a level of human oversight, running agents in robust sandboxes (with maturity models for network and compute isolation), limiting credentials, logging and monitoring agent activity, making agent traffic attributable, and keeping the ability to shut agents down immediately.
