## Summary

The near-term impact of AI on the cyber threat is an NCSC Assessment published by the UK National Cyber Security Centre on 24 January 2024 on how AI will affect the effectiveness of cyber operations and the cyber threat over the following two years, using the Professional Head of Intelligence Assessment probability yardstick. It judges that AI will almost certainly increase the volume and heighten the impact of cyber attacks, unevenly across types of threat actor, chiefly through the evolution of existing tactics and the capability uplift it gives in reconnaissance and social engineering, while more sophisticated uses remain restricted to well-resourced actors with quality training data. It also judges that AI lowers the barrier for less-skilled actors, will likely contribute to the global ransomware threat, and will become more widely available through commoditisation in criminal and commercial markets.
