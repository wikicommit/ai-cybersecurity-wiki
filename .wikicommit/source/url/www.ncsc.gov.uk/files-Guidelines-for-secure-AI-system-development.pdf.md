## Summary

Guidelines for secure AI system development is guidance published in 2023 by the UK National Cyber Security Centre (NCSC) and the US Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency (CISA) together with international partner agencies, aimed primarily at providers of systems that use artificial intelligence. It explains that AI systems are subject to novel vulnerabilities such as adversarial machine learning, prompt injection and data poisoning, and sets out considerations and mitigations across four stages of the AI system development life cycle — secure design, secure development, secure deployment, and secure operation and maintenance — following a "secure by default" approach aligned with secure-by-design principles. It closes with further reading on AI development, cyber security and risk management.
