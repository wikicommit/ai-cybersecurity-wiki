## Summary

"Thinking carefully before adopting agentic AI" is a blog post published by the UK's NCSC on 15 May 2026 that summarises the key points of Careful adoption of agentic AI services, joint guidance the NCSC co-authored with international partners. It explains that agentic AI can plan, decide and act on a user's behalf, inherits known large language model risks such as jailbreaking and prompt injection, and adds risks from broader access, unpredictable behaviour, problems that are harder to spot and actions that are harder to explain. It advises organisations to start small with tightly bounded pilots for low-risk tasks, keep humans accountable for deploying and controlling agents, and apply established cyber security practices such as least privilege, limited scope, short-lived credentials, monitoring, threat modelling and incident planning.
