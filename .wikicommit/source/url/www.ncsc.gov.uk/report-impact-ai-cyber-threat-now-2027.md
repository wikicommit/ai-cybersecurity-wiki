## Summary

Impact of AI on cyber threat from now to 2027 is an NCSC Assessment published by the UK National Cyber Security Centre on 7 May 2025, which uses the Professional Head of Intelligence Assessment probability yardstick to judge how AI developments will affect cyber threat up to 2027, focusing on the use of AI in cyber intrusion. Its key judgements are that AI will almost certainly make elements of intrusion operations more effective and efficient, that a digital divide will open between systems that keep pace with AI-enabled threats and those that do not, that AI-assisted vulnerability research and exploit development is the most significant development, and that the growing incorporation of AI systems, particularly in critical national infrastructure, presents an increased attack surface. It concludes that fundamental cyber security practices in integrating and configuring AI systems will be key to mitigating these threats.
