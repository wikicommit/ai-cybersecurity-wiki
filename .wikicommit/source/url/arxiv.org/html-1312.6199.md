## Summary

"Intriguing properties of neural networks" (Szegedy et al., arXiv:1312.6199) reports two counter-intuitive properties of deep neural networks: semantic information in high layers lies in the space of activations rather than in individual units, and the networks' input-output mappings are discontinuous enough that an imperceptible, optimised perturbation can make them misclassify an image. The paper names such perturbed inputs "adversarial examples", finds them with box-constrained L-BFGS, and shows that they transfer to networks with different hyperparameters and to networks trained on disjoint training data; it also bounds each layer's instability with a spectral (Lipschitz) analysis.
