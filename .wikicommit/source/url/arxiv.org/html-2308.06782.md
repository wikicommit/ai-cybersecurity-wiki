## Summary

"PentestGPT: Evaluating and Harnessing Large Language Models for Automated Penetration Testing" (Deng et al., arXiv:2308.06782) builds a benchmark of 13 penetration-testing targets from HackTheBox and VulnHub, broken into 182 sub-tasks, and uses it to study GPT-3.5, GPT-4 and Bard in a human-in-the-loop setting. It finds that the models handle sub-tasks such as using tools, interpreting their output and proposing next steps, but lose the overall testing context, over-focus on recent tasks and generate inaccurate commands. The authors then present PentestGPT, which splits the work across reasoning, generation and parsing modules and tracks progress in a "pentesting task tree"; they report large gains in sub-task completion over the bare models and partial success on live HackTheBox machines and a CTF competition.
