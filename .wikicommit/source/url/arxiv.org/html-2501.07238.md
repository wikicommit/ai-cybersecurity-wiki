## Summary

A January 2025 paper in which members of Microsoft's AI Red Team draw on their experience red teaming more than 100 generative AI products. It presents the threat model ontology the team uses — system, actor, tactics, techniques and procedures, weakness and impact — and eight lessons, among them that simple techniques often break AI systems as well as gradient-based ones, that AI red teaming is distinct from safety benchmarking, that automation such as the open-source PyRIT framework helps cover more of the risk landscape while human judgment remains crucial, and that securing AI systems will never be complete. Five case studies and a set of open questions for the field accompany the lessons.
