## Summary

A survey of backdoor learning that reviews backdoor attacks, which embed hidden behaviour in deep neural networks so that they act normally on benign inputs but change their predictions when an attacker-specified trigger is present, and the defences proposed against them. It sets out a unified framework and taxonomy for poisoning-based attacks, covers non-poisoning-based attacks that modify model weights or structure, groups empirical and certified defences, compares backdoor attacks with adversarial attacks and data poisoning, lists benchmark datasets, and outlines open research directions.
