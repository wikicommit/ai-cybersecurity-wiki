## Summary

A study that proposes a framework to formalise prompt injection attacks on LLM-integrated applications, defining an attack as modifying the data of a target task so that the application accomplishes an attacker-chosen injected task instead. It shows that existing attacks are special cases of the framework, designs a new Combined Attack from them, and benchmarks 5 attacks and 10 prevention- and detection-based defences with 10 LLMs and 7 tasks, finding that the attacks are effective across models and tasks and that no existing defence is sufficient.
