## Summary

An October 2024 paper that tests whether large language models can be compromised by poisoning their pre-training data, and whether the effect survives post-training alignment. The authors pre-train models from 604 million to 7 billion parameters with poisoned data for four attacks — denial-of-service, context extraction, jailbreaking and belief manipulation — and find that poisoning 0.1% of the pre-training data is enough for three of the four to persist measurably through supervised fine-tuning and DPO, with denial-of-service persisting at a poisoning rate of 0.001%. The jailbreaking backdoor did not persist through standard safety training.
