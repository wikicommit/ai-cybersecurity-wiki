## Summary

A January 2024 survey by researchers at the Hasso Plattner Institute (University of Potsdam) and Technische Universität Chemnitz that reviews the literature on large language models (LLMs) in cybersecurity. It classifies defensive applications of LLMs by the functions of the NIST Cybersecurity Framework and adversarial applications by MITRE ATT&CK tactics. It finds that most defensive studies concern the Protect and Detect functions and most attack studies concern initial access, execution and defence evasion, and names response and recovery after an attack as a research gap.
