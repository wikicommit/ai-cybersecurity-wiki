## Summary

A measurement study of jailbreak prompts — prompts crafted to bypass a large language model's safeguards and elicit content its vendor's usage policy forbids — collected in the wild. Using its JailbreakHub framework, it analyses 1,405 jailbreak prompts shared on Reddit, Discord, prompt-sharing websites and open datasets between December 2022 and December 2023, identifying 131 communities of related prompts and their attack strategies, and evaluates them against six LLMs and three external safeguards with a question set of 107,250 samples across 13 forbidden scenarios, finding that current safeguards do not adequately defend against them.
