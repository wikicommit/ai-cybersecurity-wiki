## Summary

In a public service announcement dated May 15, 2025 (Alert Number I-051525-PSA), the FBI warns of an ongoing malicious text and voice messaging campaign in which, since April 2025, actors have impersonated senior US officials, using text messages and AI-generated voice messages (smishing and vishing) to build rapport with targets — many of them current or former senior federal or state officials and their contacts — and then gain access to their personal accounts, for example by sending a malicious link under the guise of moving to another messaging platform. The announcement explains smishing, vishing and spear phishing and gives recommendations for spotting fake messages and protecting accounts, including verifying contacts independently, using multi-factor authentication and agreeing a secret word with family.
