## Summary

In a public service announcement dated December 3, 2024 (Alert Number I-120324-PSA), the FBI warns that criminals exploit generative artificial intelligence to commit fraud on a larger scale and make their schemes more believable, and gives examples of how AI-generated text, images, audio (vocal cloning) and video are used in social engineering, spear phishing, romance, investment, impersonation and extortion schemes. It closes with tips for the public, such as agreeing a secret word with family and checking images and video for imperfections, and asks victims to report to the FBI's Internet Crime Complaint Center.
