## Summary

DARPA's program page for the AI Cyber Challenge (AIxCC), a two-year competition launched in 2023 in which competitors design novel AI systems to secure the open-source software that critical infrastructure depends on, with a cumulative $29.5 million in prizes, including $7 million for small businesses. The challenge brings top AI companies together with DARPA and ARPA-H and collaborates with the open-source community; its semifinal competition was to be held at DEF CON 2024 and its final at DEF CON 2025, with a top prize of $4 million.
