## Summary

OWASP's entry LLM01:2025 Prompt Injection, from the 2025 edition of the Top 10 for LLM applications, defines a prompt injection vulnerability as user prompts altering an LLM's behaviour or output in unintended ways, distinguishes direct from indirect injections and prompt injection from jailbreaking, and lists the outcomes a successful attack can lead to, including the added risks of multimodal models. It sets out seven mitigation strategies, from constraining model behaviour to adversarial testing, nine example attack scenarios, and the related MITRE ATLAS techniques.
