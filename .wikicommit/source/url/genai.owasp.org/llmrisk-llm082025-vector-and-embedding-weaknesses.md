## Summary

OWASP's entry LLM08:2025 Vector and Embedding Weaknesses, from the 2025 edition of the Top 10 for LLM applications, describes the security risks in systems that use retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) with large language models, where weaknesses in how vectors and embeddings are generated, stored or retrieved can be exploited to inject harmful content, manipulate model outputs or access sensitive information.
