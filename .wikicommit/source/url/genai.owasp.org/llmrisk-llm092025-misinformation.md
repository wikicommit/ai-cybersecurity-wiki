This OWASP GenAI Security Project page describes LLM09:2025 Misinformation, an entry of the 2025 OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications: large language models producing false or misleading information that appears credible, with hallucination as a major cause alongside training-data bias and incomplete information, worsened by users' overreliance on the output.
