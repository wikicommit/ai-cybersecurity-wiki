## Summary

OWASP's entry LLM03:2025 Supply Chain, from the 2025 edition of the Top 10 for LLM applications, describes how LLM supply chains are exposed to vulnerabilities that can affect the integrity of training data, models and deployment platforms, extending traditional software supply-chain risks to third-party pre-trained models, fine-tuning adapters such as LoRA, collaborative model-merging services and on-device models. It lists nine common examples of risk, ten prevention and mitigation strategies (from vetting suppliers and keeping a software bill of materials to model integrity checks and red teaming), thirteen sample attack scenarios, and the related MITRE ATLAS technique ML Supply Chain Compromise.
