## Summary

OWASP's entry LLM02:2025 Sensitive Information Disclosure, from the 2025 edition of the Top 10 for LLM applications, describes the risk that LLMs, especially when embedded in applications, expose personal data, financial and health records, confidential business data, credentials or proprietary algorithms through their output. It gives three common examples (PII leakage, proprietary algorithm exposure, sensitive business data disclosure), groups its prevention strategies under sanitisation, access controls, federated learning and privacy techniques, user education, secure system configuration and advanced techniques, and lists three example attack scenarios and the related MITRE ATLAS techniques.
