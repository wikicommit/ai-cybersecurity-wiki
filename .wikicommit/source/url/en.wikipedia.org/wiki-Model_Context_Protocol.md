## Summary

The Model Context Protocol (MCP) is an open standard and open-source framework introduced by Anthropic in November 2024 to standardise how AI systems such as large language models connect to external tools, systems and data sources, through MCP hosts, clients and servers that exchange JSON-RPC 2.0 messages. The article covers its adoption by OpenAI, Google DeepMind and others, its donation to the Agentic AI Foundation under the Linux Foundation in December 2025 and the stateless revision of July 2026, and the security problems reported for it, including tool poisoning attacks, vulnerabilities in mcp-remote and MCP Inspector, and the specification's later security requirements.
