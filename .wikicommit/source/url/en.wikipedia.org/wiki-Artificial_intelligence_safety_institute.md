## Summary

An artificial intelligence safety institute (AISI) is a type of state-backed organisation that aims to evaluate and ensure the safety of advanced, or frontier, AI models. The United Kingdom and the United States created the first ones at the AI Safety Summit in November 2023, and at the AI Seoul Summit in May 2024 leaders agreed to form an international network that later became the International Network for Advanced AI Measurement, Evaluation and Science. The article describes the institutes of Australia, Canada, the European Union, France, India, Japan, Kenya, Singapore, South Korea, the United Kingdom (renamed the AI Security Institute in 2025) and the United States (renamed the Center for AI Standards and Innovation in 2025).
