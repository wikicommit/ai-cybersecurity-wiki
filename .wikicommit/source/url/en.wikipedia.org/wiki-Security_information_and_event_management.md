## Summary

This Wikipedia article describes security information and event management (SIEM), a field of computer security that combines security information management and security event management to analyse security alerts from applications and network hardware in real time, and whose systems are central to security operations centers. It covers the term's introduction by Gartner analysts in 2005, the history of log monitoring and its place in NIST guidance and regulatory frameworks, the related terminology, a SIEM's capabilities and components, use cases, and examples of correlation and alerting rules.
