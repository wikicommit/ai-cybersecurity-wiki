## Summary

garak is an open-source computer security tool, supported by Nvidia, that scans large language models and dialogue systems for security vulnerabilities and aids their penetration testing and red teaming; its name is short for "generative AI red-teaming & assessment kit". It was developed in spring 2023 by Leon Derczynski of ITU Copenhagen, first released under the GPL in June 2023 and later relicensed under Apache 2.0, and is organised around probes that run attacks, generators that wrap the target, and detectors that judge whether an attack succeeded.
