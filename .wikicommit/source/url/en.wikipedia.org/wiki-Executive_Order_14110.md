## Summary

Executive Order 14110, "Safe, Secure, and Trustworthy Development and Use of Artificial Intelligence", was signed by US President Joe Biden on 30 October 2023 and set out the administration's policy goals for AI, ordering federal agencies to act on them — including chief AI officer posts, AI security guidelines from the Department of Homeland Security, and a generative-AI companion to NIST's AI Risk Management Framework. The article covers its background, provisions, analysis and reception, and records that President Donald Trump rescinded it on 20 January 2025.
