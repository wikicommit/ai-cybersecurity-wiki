## Summary

Phishing is a form of social engineering and scam in which attackers deceive people into revealing sensitive information or installing malware; it is a common initial-access method, and large language models can automate personalized phishing at low cost. The article describes its forms (email phishing, spear phishing and whaling, vishing, smishing, QR-code phishing and adversary-in-the-middle phishing), its techniques, a history of notable attacks from the 1990s to the 2020s, and anti-phishing measures spanning user training, mail filtering and sender authentication, browser and DNS blocking, phishing-resistant authentication, takedown and legal responses.
