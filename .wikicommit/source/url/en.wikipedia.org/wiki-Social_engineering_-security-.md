## Summary

This Wikipedia article describes social engineering in information security: the use of psychological pressure to influence people to perform actions or divulge confidential information, a kind of confidence trick that exploits cognitive biases and is often one step in a larger fraud scheme. It outlines techniques such as pretexting, water holing, baiting, ad phishing, quid pro quo, scareware and tailgating, United States laws against pretexting of banking and telephone records, notable incidents from the 1970s to 2017, and training as a countermeasure.
