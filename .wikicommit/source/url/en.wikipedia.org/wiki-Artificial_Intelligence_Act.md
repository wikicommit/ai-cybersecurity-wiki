## Summary

This Wikipedia article describes the Artificial Intelligence Act, the European Union regulation (EU) 2024/1689 that establishes a common regulatory and legal framework for AI in the EU and entered into force on 1 August 2024, with provisions applying gradually over 6 to 36 months. It explains the Act's risk categories (unacceptable, high, limited and minimal risk, plus general-purpose AI), its exemptions, the governance bodies and enforcement system it creates, its penalties and regulatory sandboxes, and the legislative procedure from the 2021 Commission proposal to adoption in 2024. It closes with reactions from scholars, civil society organisations, creators' organisations and industry.
