## Summary

Voice phishing, or vishing, is the use of telephony, often Voice over IP, to conduct phishing attacks, typically to steal card numbers, bank details or other information used in identity theft. The article explains the terminology, motives and operation of vishing — its initiation mechanisms and common scams such as impersonation, tech support, parcel and kidnapping scams, including the use of audio deepfakes — and the detection, prevention and technical countermeasures against it, among them the FCC's STIR/SHAKEN mandates and AI-based call analysis. It closes with notable cases, including the IRS impersonation scam and the Swedish Mobile BankID scam.
