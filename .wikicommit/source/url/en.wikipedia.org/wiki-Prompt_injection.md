## Summary

Prompt injection is a cybersecurity exploit in which innocuous-looking inputs are designed to cause unintended behaviour in machine learning models, particularly large language models, by exploiting their inability to distinguish developer instructions from user input. The article traces the term to 2022, describes direct and indirect injection, obfuscation and prompt leaking, and recounts incidents involving Bing Chat, ChatGPT search, DeepSeek-R1, Gemini and Grok. It closes with mitigation approaches and with assessments that no surefire mitigation yet exists.
