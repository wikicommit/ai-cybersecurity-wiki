## Summary

User behavior analytics (UBA), or user and entity behavior analytics (UEBA), is the concept of analyzing the behavior of users and other subjects so that cybersecurity tools can build a profile of normal activity and highlight deviations that may indicate a compromise. The article explains why security teams use it, how UEBA (a term Gartner coined in 2015) extends UBA to devices, applications, servers and data, and how it differs from endpoint detection and response. It closes with continuous authentication, where machine-learning models check identity throughout a session from behavioral signals, and with research on its temporal patterns, evaluation and device bias.
