## Summary

The AI Safety Summit 2023 was an international conference on the safety and regulation of artificial intelligence, organised by the British government and held on 1–2 November 2023 at Bletchley Park, England, described as the first ever global summit on AI. It produced the Bletchley Declaration, in which 28 countries including the United States, China and the European Union called for international co-operation on the risks of AI, with an emphasis on frontier AI, and it led to the commissioning of the International AI Safety Report. Around it, the UK launched its AI Safety Institute and the US announced an American AI Safety Institute within NIST.
