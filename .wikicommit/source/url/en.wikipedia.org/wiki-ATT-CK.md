## Summary

MITRE ATT&CK (Adversarial Tactics, Techniques, and Common Knowledge) is a framework created by the Mitre Corporation and released in 2013 for classifying and describing cyberattacks, published as a public catalog of attacker techniques in which every technique carries an ID such as "T1566-Phishing". Its tactic categories — 14 in 2022, rising to 15 in version 19 (April 2026) when "Defense Evasion" was split into "Stealth" and "Defense Impairment" — are broken down into techniques and sub-techniques and presented in the ATT&CK Matrix for Enterprise. The framework is versioned (version 1.0 in January 2018, version 20 in October 2026), recognised by CISA's mapping best-practices guidance, and positioned as an alternative to Lockheed Martin's cyber kill chain.
