## Summary

The AI Seoul Summit 2024 was a meeting on artificial intelligence co-hosted by the South Korean and British governments on 21–22 May 2024, the second in the series that began with the AI Safety Summit at Bletchley Park in November 2023. Leaders adopted the Seoul Declaration for Safe, Innovative and Inclusive AI, committing to international co-operation on interoperable AI governance frameworks, and ministers from 27 countries and the EU adopted the Seoul Ministerial Statement for advancing AI safety. South Korea also announced plans for an AI safety research centre and an AI Safety Institute.
