## Summary

ISO/IEC JTC 1/SC 42 Artificial Intelligence is the standardization subcommittee of the ISO/IEC Joint Technical Committee 1 that develops international standards, technical reports and technical specifications for artificial intelligence; it was established in 2018, first met in Beijing that April, and has the American National Standards Institute as its secretariat. The article describes its scope as a systems integration entity, its national-body membership, its five working groups and joint working groups, its liaisons inside and outside ISO and IEC (including CEN/CENELEC JTC 21, which writes standards supporting the EU AI Act), the standards it has published, and its 2023 Lawrence D. Eicher Award.
