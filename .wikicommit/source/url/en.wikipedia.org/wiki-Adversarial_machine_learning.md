## Summary

Adversarial machine learning is the study of attacks on machine learning algorithms and of the defences against them. The article traces the field from spam-filter evasion in the mid-2000s to gradient-based attacks on deep neural networks, describes the main attack types (evasion, data poisoning, Byzantine attacks and model extraction) and techniques for generating adversarial examples, and lists proposed defence mechanisms. It also covers the challenges of applying machine learning to security, such as concept drift, unstable labels and class imbalance.
