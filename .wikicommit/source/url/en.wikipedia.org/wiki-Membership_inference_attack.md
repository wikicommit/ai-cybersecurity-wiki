## Summary

A membership inference attack (MIA) is a privacy attack that infers whether a given record was included in the data used to produce an output, such as a machine learning model or published aggregate statistics. The article traces the attack from genomic and aggregate-data studies to shadow-model, score-based and label-based attacks on machine learning models, notes its limited effectiveness against large language models, and covers defences such as differential privacy, related privacy attacks and its implications for data protection law.
