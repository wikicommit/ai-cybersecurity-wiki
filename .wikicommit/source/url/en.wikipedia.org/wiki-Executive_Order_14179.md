## Summary

Executive Order 14179, "Removing Barriers to American Leadership in Artificial Intelligence", is a United States executive order signed by President Donald Trump on January 23, 2025, as the replacement for Joe Biden's Executive Order 14110, which Trump had rescinded on his first day in office. It revokes AI policies seen as barriers to innovation, orders an AI action plan within 180 days, and directs agencies and the Office of Management and Budget to review and revise actions taken under the earlier order; the subsequent request for public input drew over 10,000 comments, including submissions from OpenAI and Google.
