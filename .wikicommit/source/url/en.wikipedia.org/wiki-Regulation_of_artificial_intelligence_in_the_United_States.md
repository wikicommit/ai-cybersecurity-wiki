## Summary

This Wikipedia article surveys how the United States federal government and the states regulate artificial intelligence through executive orders, federal and state laws and sector-specific agency rules. It traces federal policy from the Obama administration's 2016 reports through the Biden administration's voluntary company commitments, AI Bill of Rights and Executive Order 14110, to the second Trump administration's revocation of that order, its push to preempt state AI laws (Executive Order 14365), the creation of the Center for AI Standards and Innovation, export controls and Executive Order 14409 on frontier AI security, and it describes state laws in California, Colorado, New York, Tennessee, Texas and Utah.
