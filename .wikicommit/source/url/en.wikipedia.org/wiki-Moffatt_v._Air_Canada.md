## Summary

Moffatt v. Air Canada (2024 BCCRT 149) is a February 2024 decision of British Columbia's Civil Resolution Tribunal holding that a company can be liable for misleading information its AI chatbot gives customers. After the airline's website chatbot wrongly told a bereaved customer that a bereavement fare could be claimed retroactively, the tribunal rejected the airline's argument that the chatbot was a separate legal entity, found it liable for negligent misrepresentation and awarded the price difference plus costs and interest. The article notes the international attention the small-claims case received as one of the earliest to hold a company liable for its AI agent's mistakes.
