## Summary

The 2016 Cyber Grand Challenge (CGC) was a two-year competition launched by DARPA in 2014 to create fully automated defensive systems able to find, prove and patch software flaws in real time, culminating in an all-machine capture-the-flag final held on 4 August 2016 at DEF CON 24 in Las Vegas. The article describes the challenge binaries and the 'cyber reasoning systems' the teams built, the June 2015 qualification event in which 28 teams took part, and the final, won by ForAllSecure's Mayhem ahead of TECHx's Xandra and Shellphish's Mechanical Phish.
