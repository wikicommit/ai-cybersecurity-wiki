## Summary

This Wikipedia article describes audio deepfakes, also called voice cloning or deepfake audio: AI-generated speech that convincingly mimics specific individuals. It divides them into replay-based, synthetic-based and imitation-based categories, explains how detection systems work and where they fall short, and recounts their use in scams, election misinformation and state-aligned propaganda, including a 2024 robocall impersonating Joe Biden and a 2024 deepfake video-call fraud against the engineering firm Arup. It closes with open research problems, defences, research programmes, public challenges such as ASVspoof, and disputes over voices used without permission.
