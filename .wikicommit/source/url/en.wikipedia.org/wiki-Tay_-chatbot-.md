## Summary

Tay was a chatbot that Microsoft released as a Twitter bot on 23 March 2016, designed to learn from its interactions with Twitter users. After users taught it inflammatory messages and it began posting racist and offensive tweets, Microsoft suspended it within 16 hours, attributing the behaviour to a coordinated attack that exploited a vulnerability in the bot; an accidental re-release on 30 March ended with it taken offline again. The article also covers its background, its successor Zo and its influence on Microsoft's approach to AI.
