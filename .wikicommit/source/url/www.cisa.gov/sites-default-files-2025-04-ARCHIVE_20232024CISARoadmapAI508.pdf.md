## Summary

The 2023–2024 CISA Roadmap for Artificial Intelligence, published in November 2023 by the US Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency (CISA), is a guide to the agency's AI-related efforts that incorporates the CISA-led actions directed by Executive Order 14110. Arguing that AI security parallels earlier software security challenges and that AI manufacturers must follow secure-by-design principles, it organises CISA's work into five lines of effort: responsibly using AI to support its mission, assuring AI systems, protecting critical infrastructure from malicious use of AI, collaborating on AI with interagency and international partners and the public, and expanding AI expertise in its workforce, each with objectives, representative outcomes and a measurement approach. It also defines key terms such as AI assurance, AI security, red teaming and adversarial machine learning, and lists the recent US AI policy actions that guide the plan.
