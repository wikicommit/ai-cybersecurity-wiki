Executive Order 14409, "Promoting Advanced Artificial Intelligence Innovation and Security", is a United States executive order issued by President Donald Trump in June 2026. It directs federal agencies to strengthen federal cybersecurity, expand the use of AI-enabled defensive tools, establish a voluntary framework for the secure deployment of frontier AI models, and prioritize enforcement against AI-enabled cybercrime.

## What it directs

| Area | Direction to agencies |
| --- | --- |
| Federal cybersecurity | Strengthen it |
| AI for defense | Expand the use of AI-enabled defensive tools |
| Frontier AI models | Establish a voluntary framework for their secure deployment |
| Cybercrime | Prioritize enforcement against AI-enabled cybercrime |

## The secure-deployment framework

The framework for secure deployment includes a "covered frontier model" designation. The designation is applied by the Director of the National Security Agency (NSA), in accordance with a "classified benchmarking process", to assess the capabilities of AI models. AI developers may voluntarily submit newly developed models to the federal government to determine whether they qualify as covered frontier models, and to give the federal government exclusive access to them for up to thirty days before they are released more broadly.

## Context

The order is part of the second Trump administration's AI policy, which began in January 2025 with the revocation of [[Legislation/executive-order-14110|Executive Order 14110]] and the issuing of [[Legislation/executive-order-14179|Executive Order 14179]], and which in December 2025 included [[Legislation/executive-order-14365|Executive Order 14365]] on a national policy framework intended to preempt state regulation. In the same month as the order, June 2026, the Department of Commerce extended export controls beyond AI hardware by requiring licenses for certain frontier AI models from Anthropic, while authorizing their release to specified trusted partners.
