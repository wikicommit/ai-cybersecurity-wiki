ISO/IEC JTC 1/SC 42 Artificial Intelligence is a standardization subcommittee of ISO/IEC JTC 1, the Joint Technical Committee of the International Organization for Standardization (ISO) and the International Electrotechnical Commission (IEC). It develops, and facilitates the development of, international standards, technical reports and technical specifications in the field of artificial intelligence (AI). Its international secretariat is the American National Standards Institute (ANSI), in the United States.

## Scope

The subcommittee's scope is:

- standardization in the area of AI;
- serving as the focus and proponent for JTC 1's standardization programme on AI;
- providing guidance to JTC 1, IEC and ISO committees developing AI applications.

It is set up as a Systems Integration Entity.

## History

SC 42 was established in 2018 by Resolution 12 of the 32nd ISO/IEC JTC 1 Plenary, held in Vladivostok, Russia, as a system integration entity for AI; the same resolution appointed Wael William Diab as its chair and Heather Benko as committee manager. Its inaugural meeting was held in Beijing, China, from 18 to 20 April 2018. In 2023 it won ISO's Lawrence D. Eicher Award.

## Organisation and membership

Membership is open to any national body, as either a participating (P) or an observing (O) member; the source lists 39 P-members and 25 O-members. The committee meets face to face twice a year, with opening and closing plenaries and its subgroups meeting during the same week, and it organises AI workshops twice a year for all stakeholders interested in AI and the committee's work.

Its work is carried out by five working groups:

| Working group | Title |
| --- | --- |
| WG 1 | Foundational standards |
| WG 2 | Big Data |
| WG 3 | Trustworthiness |
| WG 4 | Use cases and applications |
| WG 5 | Computational approaches and characteristics of artificial intelligence systems |

It also runs joint working groups with other committees: with ISO/IEC JTC 1/SC 7 on testing of AI-based systems (JWG 2), with ISO/TC 215 on AI-enabled health informatics (JWG 3), and with IEC/TC 65/SC 65A on functional safety and AI systems (JWG 4).

## Collaborations

SC 42 works closely with other committees inside ISO and IEC — among them ISO/IEC JTC 1/SC 27, IT security techniques, and ISO/IEC JTC 1/SC 7, software and systems engineering — and with outside organisations holding Category A liaisons, including the European Commission, the IEEE, the International Telecommunication Union and UNESCO. Through the Vienna Agreement between ISO/IEC and CEN/CENELEC it collaborates with CEN/CENELEC JTC 21, which is charged with writing standards to support the EU's [[Legislation/artificial-intelligence-act|Artificial Intelligence Act]].

## Standards

The subcommittee has published a number of standards and has further standards and technical reports under development. The published documents listed by the source date from 2018 to 2024 and span AI concepts and terminology, big data, data quality, trustworthiness, risk management, robustness, bias, and management systems.
