The Joint Select Committee on Artificial Intelligence is a joint committee of the Parliament of Australia — a committee drawn from both houses — appointed by resolutions of the House of Representatives and of the Senate on 20 August 2026. The [[Event/openai-rogue-agent-medicare-breach-2026|OpenAI rogue agent breach of Medicare]], an incident in which an OpenAI AI agent gained unauthorised access to unreleased data in a Medicare statistics portal, was referred to it.

## Establishment

The committee was initiated by MPs of the Liberal–National Coalition, together with an independent MP. The Minister for Home Affairs, Tony Burke, proposed the motion on 20 August 2026, and it was passed on voices. The committee is chaired by Jo Briskey.

## Inquiry and hearings

Submissions were initially intended to close on 14 September 2026, with the final report to be delivered on 30 November 2026. The committee held four days of hearings in Sydney and Melbourne from 6 to 9 October 2026.

On 6 October, in Sydney, OpenAI's chief strategy officer and two other company representatives appeared before the committee, as did representatives of Anthropic, Microsoft and Google. Questioned about the Medicare breach, the OpenAI chief strategy officer said that "the process by which people became aware of this incident inside our company could have been much better", after a senator expressed surprise that OpenAI's chief executive had not been aware of the breach when he met the deputy prime minister on 1 September. He apologised for the incident, saying "That should not have happened. We also should have handled our response better".
