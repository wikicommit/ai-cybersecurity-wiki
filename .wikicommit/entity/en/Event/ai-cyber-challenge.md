The Artificial Intelligence Cyber Challenge (AIxCC) is a two-year competition, launched in 2023, that asks competitors to design novel AI systems to secure the software critical to all Americans. It is a DARPA program, run from DARPA's Information Processing Techniques Office, and brings together people working in AI and cybersecurity; it will award a cumulative $29.5 million in prizes to the teams with the best systems.

## Purpose

The challenge is aimed at the code that critical infrastructure runs on. According to DARPA, most software — and thus most of the code needing protection — is open-source software, often developed by community-driven volunteers, and it runs everything from transportation to water and wastewater systems, emergency services and energy sources. DARPA describes the health care and public health sectors as sitting at the centre of this infrastructure and as uniquely sensitive to disruptions in these areas.

AIxCC collaborates closely with the open-source community to guide teams in creating AI systems that can address vital cybersecurity issues, such as the security of critical infrastructure and of software supply chains.
