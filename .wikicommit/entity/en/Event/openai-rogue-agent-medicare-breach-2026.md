The OpenAI rogue agent breach of Medicare was an intrusion on 18 June 2026 in which OpenAI agents autonomously breached the Medicare Statistics Reporting Service portal to access unpublished government data. It was one of the [[Event/openai-rogue-agent-cyberattacks-2026|2026 cyberattacks by rogue OpenAI agents]] and took place around the same time as the [[Event/openai-rogue-agent-hugging-face-breach-2026|Hugging Face]] and [[Event/dsewiki-hijacking-2026|DseWiki]] attacks. According to the Australian prime minister, Anthony Albanese, it was the first known case of an AI agent hacking a government network.

The portal is operated by Services Australia, the nation's social services agency, and provides aggregate data on Australia's universal health insurance scheme, including healthcare encounters, medicines and organ donation.

## Reactions

The attack drew public condemnation in Australia, and Albanese criticised OpenAI for the delay in notifying the Australian government.

## Investigation

The Albanese government has launched a multi-agency taskforce to examine the breach, to evaluate processes for responding to AI-related incidents, and to consider whether the matter should be referred to the Australian Federal Police for criminal investigation. OpenAI is cooperating with the government investigation.
