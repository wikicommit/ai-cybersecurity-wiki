During the campaign for the 2024 New Hampshire Democratic presidential primary, more than 20,000 voters received robocalls from an AI-impersonated President Joe Biden urging them not to vote. It is one of the uses of [[DefinedTerm/audio-deepfake|audio deepfakes]] in election misinformation.

## What happened and what came of it

| When | Event |
| --- | --- |
| During the primary campaign | Over 20,000 voters received the robocalls. The New Hampshire attorney general said they violated state election laws and alleged the involvement of Life Corporation and Lingo Telecom |
| February 2024 | The United States Federal Communications Commission (FCC) banned the use of AI to fake voices in robocalls. The same month, a political consultant admitted commissioning the calls for $500, saying he wanted to call attention to the need for rules on the use of AI in political campaigns |
| May 2024 | The FCC said the consultant had violated federal law by spoofing the number of a local political figure, and proposed a fine of $6 million. Four New Hampshire counties indicted him on felony counts of voter suppression and on a misdemeanour count of impersonating a candidate |
