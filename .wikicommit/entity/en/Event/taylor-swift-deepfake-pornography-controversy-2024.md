In late January 2024, sexually explicit AI-generated deepfake images of Taylor Swift spread on X; one post was reported to have been seen over 47 million times before it was removed. The incident is one of the controversies over the misuse of AI image generators.

## Origin of the images

The disinformation research firm Graphika traced the images back to 4chan. Members of a Telegram group had discussed ways to get around the censorship safeguards of AI image generators in order to create pornographic images of celebrities.

## Response

| When | Response |
| --- | --- |
| After the images spread | Anti-sexual-assault advocacy groups, US politicians and Swifties responded publicly. Microsoft CEO Satya Nadella called the incident "alarming and terrible" |
| January 27, 2024 | X briefly blocked searches of Swift's name |
| After the incident | Microsoft enhanced the safeguards of its text-to-image model to prevent future abuse |
| January 30, 2024 | US senators Dick Durbin, Lindsey Graham, Amy Klobuchar and Josh Hawley introduced a bipartisan bill that would let victims sue individuals who produced or possessed "digital forgeries" with intent to distribute them, or who received the material knowing it was made without consent |
