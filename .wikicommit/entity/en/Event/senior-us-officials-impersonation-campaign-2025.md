The senior US officials impersonation campaign is a malicious text and voice messaging campaign in which actors impersonated senior US officials, using text messages and AI-generated voice messages, to gain access to the personal accounts of the people they targeted. The FBI disclosed it in a public service announcement, [[Report/senior-us-officials-impersonated-in-malicious-messaging-campaign]], on May 15, 2025, and described it at that date as ongoing.

## How the campaign worked

According to the FBI, the actors sent text messages and AI-generated voice messages — techniques known as smishing and [[DefinedTerm/voice-phishing|vishing]] — claiming to come from a senior US official, in an effort to establish rapport before gaining access to personal accounts. One way they gained such access was by sending targeted individuals a malicious link under the guise of transitioning to a separate messaging platform.

## Who was targeted

Many of the individuals targeted were current or former senior US federal or state government officials and their contacts. The FBI warned that access to personal or official accounts operated by US officials could be used to target other government officials, or their associates and contacts, using the trusted contact information obtained, and that contact information acquired this way could be used to impersonate contacts to elicit information or funds.

## Response

The FBI's announcement told the public not to assume that a message claiming to be from a senior US official is authentic, gave recommendations for spotting fake messages and protecting accounts, and asked victims to contact their relevant security officials and the FBI.
