The Arup deepfake scam was a fraud in Hong Kong in which fake voices and images were used against the British engineering firm Arup. Arup confirmed in May 2024 that it had been the target. It is one of the scams carried out with [[DefinedTerm/audio-deepfake|audio deepfakes]].

## What happened

According to Hong Kong police, a finance employee joined a video call with deepfake re-creations of the company's chief financial officer and other colleagues, and then transferred 200 million Hong Kong dollars (about US$25.6 million) to the fraudsters in 15 transactions.
