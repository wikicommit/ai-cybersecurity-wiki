The AI Seoul Summit 2024 was an international meeting on artificial intelligence held on 21–22 May 2024 and co-hosted by the South Korean and British governments. It was the second such meeting, following the [[Event/ai-safety-summit-2023|AI Safety Summit]] held in the United Kingdom in November 2023, and was followed by the AI Action Summit. At the summit, the Seoul Declaration was adopted to address AI technology and its related challenges and opportunities.

## Background

In the Bletchley Declaration issued at the 2023 summit, the participating countries agreed to prioritise identifying AI safety risks of shared concern. According to the article, at the Seoul summit the leaders also recognised the importance of AI.

## Who attended

The summit was attended by the leaders of Group of Seven countries, including the United States, Canada, France and Germany, and of South Korea, Singapore and Australia, together with representatives of the United Nations, the Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development (OECD) and the European Union. Representatives of global companies also attended, among them Tesla chief executive Elon Musk, Samsung Electronics chairman Lee Jae-yong, and OpenAI, Google, Microsoft, Meta and the South Korean portal operator Naver.

## South Korea's AI safety plans

South Korean President Yoon Suk Yeol said that South Korea would push forward with establishing an AI safety research centre in the country and join a network to boost the global safety of AI. The Minister of Science, Lee Jong-ho, said South Korea planned to open an AI Safety Institute in 2024, and expressed his intention to strengthen co-operation on developing international standards.
