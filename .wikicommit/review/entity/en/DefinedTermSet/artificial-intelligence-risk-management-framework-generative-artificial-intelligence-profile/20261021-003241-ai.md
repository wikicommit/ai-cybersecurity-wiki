In the twelve-risks table, the 'Dangerous, Violent, or Hateful Content' row reads as if the profile describes 'eased production of and access to' hateful, disparaging or stereotyping content. The source says something narrower. Its definition (nvlpubs NIST.AI.600-1, risk 3) covers eased production of violent or inciting content, but for hateful content it says the risk 'Includes difficulty controlling public exposure to hateful and disparaging or stereotyping content.' Section 2.3 repeats this framing: 'difficulty controlling creation of and public exposure to offensive or hateful language'. The row's wording is close, but it moves the hateful-content part away from controlling exposure and toward ease of production.
