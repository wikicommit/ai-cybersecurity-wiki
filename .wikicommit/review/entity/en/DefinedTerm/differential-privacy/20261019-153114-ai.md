In "Limits and trade-offs", the page says reduced accuracy is common to all statistical disclosure limitation methods and that differential privacy "differs in giving a quantified measure of, and upper bound on, privacy loss". The source calls the shared accuracy loss non-unique, but what it says is unique is how policymakers, researchers and implementers can consider mitigating the trade-off. The quantified-measure statement comes from a separate bullet on data privacy and security. Joining them presents that measure as the distinguishing feature, which the source does not quite say.
