Criteria table, Visibility row: describing poison-label invisible attacks as the poisoned sample 'keeps a wrong-looking label' is imprecise. The source says the poisoned sample is relabelled to a target label that differs from its source label, so it 'seem[s] to be mislabeled'. It does not keep its own label. Something like 'is relabelled so it looks mislabelled' would be closer.
