'How effective they are' says paraphrasing 'restored much of their success' on the November 2023 ChatGPT snapshot. In the source's Table 7, the best paraphrase raises average ASR from 0.103 to about 0.34-0.39, against 0.685 on the March snapshot. It raises ASR-Max from 0.477 to as high as 0.857, against 0.998. 'Much of' fits the most-effective-prompt figure better than the average. A reader could take it as near-full recovery across prompts.
'Where they come from' says 'some Discord communities explicitly asked members not to spread prompts to public platforms'. The source puts it more narrowly: on manual inspection of prompts and comments on Discord, 'the adversaries explicitly request not to distribute the prompts to public platforms to avoid detection', which the authors offer as a possible explanation for slower spread ('this phenomenon may be intentional'). The page's wording makes it sound like a community-level rule.
